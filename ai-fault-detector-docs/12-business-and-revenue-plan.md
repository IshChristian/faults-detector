## 6. Pilot offer
- 14-day pilot: 50,000 RWF.
- If the customer subscribes, the pilot fee is credited to setup.
- Pilot includes one camera, two rules, dashboard, and Telegram alerts.

## 7. Customer onboarding process
1. Site survey.
2. Choose faults and KPIs.
3. Connect cameras/screens.
4. Configure zones and rules.
5. Run 7-day shadow mode (rules record events but send no alerts).
6. Review accuracy with customer.
7. Activate alerts.
8. Train staff; install privacy signage; sign the data processing agreement.
9. Start monthly subscription.

## 8. Key selling metrics
- Faster incident response.
- Evidence clip for every alert.
- Reduced queue waiting.
- Improved table turnover.
- Reduced after-hours risk.
- Early detection of leaks, smoke, and equipment faults.
- Sports training and match review.
