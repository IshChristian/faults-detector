## 1. Objective
Install an AI monitoring system that detects important operational and security events from your existing or new cameras/screens and alerts the right person immediately.

## 2. What we monitor
Choose the modules relevant to your business:

- Security: intrusion, restricted-area entry, loitering, after-hours movement.
- Restaurant: queue length, table occupancy, kitchen safety, staff presence.
- Sports: pitch intrusion, crowd safety, player/ball tracking, training review.
- Equipment: leaks, smoke, stopped machines, missing guards.
- Screens: frozen dashboards, black screens, error messages.

## 3. How it works
1. We connect your cameras or screen sources.
2. We define zones and fault rules.
3. AI monitors the live feed.
4. When a fault persists, the system saves evidence and sends an alert.
5. Your team reviews and responds.
6. We improve accuracy using feedback.

## 4. What you receive
- Web dashboard.
- Live camera view.
- Event alerts with snapshots/clips.
- Telegram/email notifications.
- Daily or weekly report.
- Model updates during the subscription.
- Basic support.

## 7. Important notes
- AI improves with customer-specific footage and feedback.
- The system assists human decision-making; it does not replace responsible staff.
- Alerts may occasionally be false positives, especially during the first weeks.
- Camera placement, lighting, and internet quality affect performance.

## 8. Data protection

- No facial recognition.
- Both parties register with NCSA as required by Law No. 058/2021; a data processing agreement is attached.
